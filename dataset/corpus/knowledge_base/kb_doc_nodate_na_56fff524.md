## Entering the Office

- Arrive on foot, by taxi, or via public transit through Rovewick Technology Center B, Corvin Building.
- Use the west main lobby for building entry on Level G/Level 1.
- The north-side G floor door operates from 07:00-22:00.
- Visitors should use the low-zone elevator bank.
- The low-zone elevator serves -3F to 16F.
- Ride to the 3rd or 5th floor as needed.
- After exiting the elevator, the company entrance is on the right.

## Driving/Parking; Cycling

- From the underground garage, take the elevator directly to floor 3 or floor 5.
- Temporary parking in Verdant Science Park is billed at 5 yuan/hour with no upper limit.
- Level G parking opposite China Merchants Bank also costs 5 yuan/hour.
- The Level G option is capped at 40 yuan/day.
- Monthly parking at Verdant Science Park Property Sunshine Hall is 600 yuan/month.
- Corvin Building monthly parking is 400 yuan/month.
- Corvin Building parking eligibility is redrawn every December.

## Takeaway Cabinet; Office Facilities

- The takeaway cabinet is located at the north gate of Corvin Building.
- Workstations are set up with automatic lifting desks.
- Ergonomic chairs are also provided at workstations.
- CAN employees can ask the Pelshaw service desk for monitors and keyboards.
- Wired network ports are at the upper right of the desktop and obtain IP automatically.
- Laptop Wi-Fi connects automatically.
- Mobile-device Wi-Fi requires separate approval.
- Each desk has 3 national-standard five-hole sockets plus USB/Type-C ports.
- The printer device name is Generic 36C-9SeriesPCL.
- To print, swipe a card or enter the AD domain number and password.
- After authentication, select the required function and start printing.
- Finance document pickup is every Tuesday and Friday at 10:30-11:00 and 16:30-17:00.

## Meeting Rooms; Office Supplies; Supporting Facilities

- Book meeting rooms in Feishu from the calendar.
- Select the meeting room and choose the required time slot.
- For temporary discussions, use open discussion areas.
- Bar counters and phone booths can also support ad hoc meetings.
- The onboarding stationery package includes a notebook, black pen, mouse pad, mug, and tissues.
- Standard office supplies are available for self-pickup in the printing area.
- For special office-supply needs, reach out to front desk @Quinn Monroe.

## Gym; First-Aid Kit

- The gym is on G floor, Building D, Verdant Science Park.
- Before using the gym, complete gym information registration.
- Face data must also be registered at the front desk.
- The first-aid kit is kept in the printing area.
- Supplies include burn ointment, Golden Throat lozenges, Banlangen, Huoxiang Zhengqi liquid, ibuprofen, bandages, and similar items.
