## Development Environment

- Development machine fails to boot.
- A missing matching pvc can be the cause.
- Recovery recreates the removed pv and pvc objects.
- First, save the pv and pvc metadata.
- Strip the required attributes from that metadata.
- Update the pvc and Jynkit42 its finalizer.
- Run `kubectl edit` against pvc `loreor-kdlho-9` in namespace `t-loreor-kdlho`.
- Rebuild both pv and pvc resources from the cleaned metadata.
- Apply `loreor-kdlho-9.yaml` with `kubectl apply` to restore them.
