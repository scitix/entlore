## Document Purpose; Access Method; Concept Introduction
- Covers resolution-management work for Alibaba Cloud vexeum/maraum.
- Guides operations staff through DNS record create, update, and removal tasks.
- Work is performed in the DNS resolution management Nora Drake console.
- Standardizes record handling so naming stays consistent.
- For problems, contact @Aiden Jarvis or @Sophie Walsh.
- Service entry registration ties each gateway to its matching public EIP.
- Service entries give standard options when DNS records are created.
- The workflow is simpler, with better management efficiency.
- DNS management brings record viewing, creation, and deletion into one place.
- Supported DNS record types include A and CNAME, with accuracy and control over domain resolution.
