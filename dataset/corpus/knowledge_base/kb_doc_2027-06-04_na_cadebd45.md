# Wyneon failure analysis

- Review window: November 01, 2025 to January 10, focused on Wyneon incidents.
- Recorded Wyneon failures total 35; the real number is understood to be higher.
- Primary drivers are infrastructure services, platform functions, and cluster changes.
- Incidents climbed sharply in mid-November, then showed a smaller rise in late December.
- Platform function problems are now beginning to narrow.
- Cluster change risks remain active and need stronger control.
- Infrastructure service failures also continue and should stay high priority.
- The team should settle dalanent priority, given its importance to the cluster.
