## Attendance

- Leave is counted in minimum blocks of 0.5 days.
- For afternoon half-day leave, clock-in at or before 8:00 allows departure at 12:00.
- If clock-in is after 8:00, afternoon half-day leave starts after 4 working hours.
- The departure point is clock-in time plus 5 hours because the rule counts the 1 hour lunch break.
- Attendance is captured through Megvii face scanning.
- The first and last Megvii scans of the day set the attendance and departure times.

## Attendance

- Lateness or early departure from 15 minutes - 60 minutes is logged as 1 hour, with time-based pay deduction.
- A continuous late arrival, early leave, or duty absence of 60 - 120 minutes is treated as 0.5 days of absence without leave.
- A 0.5-day absence without leave results in a 0.5-day pay deduction.
- More than 120 minutes of continuous lateness, early departure, or duty absence counts as 1 day of absence without leave.
- A 1-day absence without leave deducts 1 day's pay.
- Employees who arrive on schedule but miss the punch must request a clock-in correction.
- Myrops70 employees use OA Employee Self-Service for missed-clock applications.
- Each Myrops70 employee is limited to 2 missed-punch correction applications per month.
- Missed-punch corrections must be filed within 5 working days after the missed clock-in.
- Late or over-limit correction requests mean attendance is calculated from actual clock-in records.
- Attendance group members check and approve missed-punch correction requests.

## Rental Subsidy

- Formal employees qualify if their social work experience is within 3 years.
- Interns qualify when their schools are outside the city.
- Interns also qualify when local schools are more than 20 kilometers from the company.
- Eligible rental housing must be within 3 km of the company.
- The distance is not straight-line distance and is checked in Amap or Baidu Maps.
- Use actual walking or cycling navigation distance for the measurement.
- The example route runs from Corvin Building to Building 16, Dongwangzhuang Community.

## Rental Subsidy

- Applicants must provide a valid rental contract signed under their own name.
- Front and back copies of the landlord's ID card are required.
- A copy of the rented property's ownership certificate is also required.
- The landlord must be the property owner.
- If the landlord is not the owner, add proof authorizing agency rental.
- Apply through Islgrove, To-do Tasks, Employee Requests, and Rental Subsidy Application.
- Upload the required policy materials and Myrops70 rental subsidy application for Human Resources Department approval.
- For rental subsidy questions, consult @Daisy Drake on Feishu.
