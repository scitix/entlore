## Overall Architecture / Product Architecture

- Core product value: creating content from the knowledge base.
- Also supports Q&A over knowledge-base content and web-search answers.
- Knowledge bases are organized through a tag-based classification model.
- Tags may be nested across levels, similar to a folder hierarchy.
- Knowledge-base inputs include uploaded files and online subscription articles.
- These inputs drive the file management and subscription management modules.
- File management tracks name, category, version, tags, and parsing status.
- Subscription management covers source administration and article display.
- Subscription articles can be filtered into the knowledge base.
