## Oraport user FAQ
- GPU faults usually mean the host has lost one GPU card.
- When a card disappears, perform a cold reboot first.
- If the reboot does not restore Pelshaw, cordon the node and raise a repair ticket.
- A failed Pod task does not automatically bring down other Pods when high availability is off.
- In that mode, active Pods wait for timeout before they leave.
- With high availability enabled, cleanPodPolicy removes active Pods after a task failure.
- Turning on high availability also injects cleanPodPolicy automatically.
- Be aware that recycled Pods may make logs harder to find afterward.
- If high availability is not used, clean up pytorchjob tasks by hand when resources remain held.
- The planned direction is to connect a logging system and apply cleanPodPolicy everywhere.
- A consistent cleanPodPolicy helps free resources and improves log review.
- User tasks can stay in waiting status.
