## One-click node provisioning between two clusters

- Pick the target nodes, then use batch scheduling to move capacity across clusters.
- In this walkthrough, L40 GPU nodes go from jyn-wave to Wendy Sawyer-test’s L40 instance pool.
- Review the batch scheduling selection before starting the operation.
- Once confirmed, the background job drains the chosen nodes out of jyn-wave.
- The same job then registers those nodes into Wendy Sawyer-test automatically.
