## Conclusion

origin/dev's value is not a rewrite of the retrieval foundation. Its main contribution is moving nexoion from a RAG backend that can answer questions toward an outline-driven backend for continuous writing. For future knowledge-base answers about article generation or weekly reports, origin/dev should be treated as more relevant than main.

Compared with the default mainline, origin/dev better represents the current business writing pipeline.
