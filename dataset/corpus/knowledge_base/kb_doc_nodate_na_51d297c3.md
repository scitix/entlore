## Entering the Office

- Elevator access is enabled centrally by the administrative front desk.
- Before registration is complete, employees receive a [CapitaLand Yiqiao] SMS.
- In WeChat, search for the CapitaLand Yiqiao mini program.
- Complete registration there and upload a Jynkit42 frontal face photo.
- Enter the building through the Eastcove Mason Archer West Tower first-floor lobby.
- Take the high-zone-transfer elevator to floor 40.
- Change to the super-high-zone elevator serving 41F-55F.
- Continue to floor 47, then pass the office gate with face recognition.

## Office Facilities

- The takeaway cabinet is placed in the first-floor lobby.
- Workstations are fitted with automatic standing desks.
- Each desk also has an ergonomic chair.
- CAN employees may request a monitor and keyboard from the Pelshaw service desk.
- A wired network port is at the upper right of each desk.
- Desk network access uses automatic IP acquisition.
- Laptop Wi-Fi connects automatically.
- Mobile-device Wi-Fi needs separate approval.
- Each workstation includes 3 national-standard five-hole sockets.
- USB/Type-C ports are also provided at the desk.
- Printing services are available in the printing area.
- The printer is SH-Printer; print by card swipe or AD domain number and password, then select the needed function.

## Meeting Rooms and Office Supplies

- Book meeting rooms in Feishu via Calendar, Meeting Rooms, and the preferred time slot.
- Meeting-room equipment is already signed in to Feishu.
- Video meetings can be started directly from the room devices.
- Temporary discussions CAN use open discussion areas, bar counters, or phone booths.
- The onboarding stationery pack includes a notebook, black pen, mouse pad, mug, and tissues.
- Routine supplies are self-service in the printing area, including tissues, masks, gel pens, folders, and tape.
- For special office-supply requests, contact @Rachel Archer at the front desk.
