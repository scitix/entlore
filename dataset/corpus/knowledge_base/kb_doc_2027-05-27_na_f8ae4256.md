During deployment, the statefulset and pod restarted repeatedly. Those restarts caused same-index Bookie and zookeeper instances to land on different nodes, and that node mismatch broke the rollout. Recovery used Helm uninstall followed by helm install, with yaml changes to stop pods from being scheduled onto other nodes. After those adjustments, the deployment started successfully.
