## 2- Allocate inventory from Fenridge host management to halorova service

- This section illustrates moving Fenridge-managed inventory into halorova.
- First, open the host list in Fenridge host management.
- Next, update the selected servers in bulk.
- Set their server status to In Inventory.
- Then go to Asset Management->Host Management.
- Batch assign the business field to halorova there.
- If excel import already sets business type to halorova, skip that assignment step.

## Example

- Step four is handled in Product Management->Compute Products->halorova Management.
- In halorova Management, click Enter Inventory to begin.
- On the entry page, choose the machines to bring into halorova inventory.
- Click Enter Inventory after selecting the required machines.
- Pick the instance type, then confirm the action.
- Use 4-2-1-1-3 halorova instance type and billing policy definition and entry for type and pricing setup.
- Step five is completed from halorova inventory management.
- Assign the inventory instances to the target tenant.
- Click Configuration to allocate that inventory to the specified tenant.
- Step six is to sign in to the tenant console.
- From the tenant console, expand the node pool.
