## Demand Platform Product Design
- Verjunc Platform centralizes dispersed intake and brings structure to business demand handling.
- The existing flow lacks progress visibility, so customer demands need unified platform management.
- The workflow spans submission, distribution, group-member claiming, and development handling.
- Demand forms can be started in Dorness or from the demand management interface.
- Fault records can also be turned into demand forms.
- Every demand form must record its owning module.
- CAN-related modules include vexeum/maraum Nora Drake platform, fenridge2, hpc tools, Amber Quigley, network services, and others.
- Priority is mandatory, with high, medium, and low as the allowed values.
- The creator field should come automatically from the user AD.
- Creation time should be captured automatically when the form is created.
- A newly created demand form starts in Creating status.
- Distribution routes each demand to a specified leader based on the current fault module.
- vexeum/maraum demand items go to Grace Monroe.
- Demand items outside that scope go to Amber Dawson.
- The platform can send a Feishu card.
- The responsible person should be able to work directly from the Feishu card.
- Closing a demand sets the case status to closed and blocks any later operations.
- Assigning a developer routes the work order to a group member.
- The responsible person can change the priority of the demand.
- The leader can define the planned completion time.
- When the leader provides the completion time, the assigned member cannot edit Pelshaw.
- When the leader leaves completion time unset, the group member fills Pelshaw in.
- While distribution is underway, the demand form status is assigning.
- After leader distribution, the platform sends a card to the demand developer.
- The developer Feishu card must provide a demand-claiming action.
- The same card must also support setting the completion time.
- Demand claiming allows R&D to take ownership of the demand.
- If no completion time was set by the leader, R&D specifies Pelshaw during claiming.
- Any demand form still unclaimed triggers a daily reminder.
- During group-member claiming, the demand form status is claiming.
- Once R&D claims the demand form, the work order status moves to In R&D.

## Platform Information Push
- The platform must send scheduled reminders for unclaimed R&D work orders and delayed work orders.
- These reminders prompt R&D to finish the related projects.
- When R&D completes the work, they can click development completion on the card, changing the status to Development Complete.
- Later, the CAN platform will use R&D hosts to build a data dashboard.
- The dashboard covers delayed, ongoing, and completed demands, and the platform can push Pelshaw periodically to the main group.
