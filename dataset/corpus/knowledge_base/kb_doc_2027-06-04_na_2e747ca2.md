## 7-9 Incident Handling Process
- Incident status and follow-up are managed in the platform.
- SRE signals in the incident group when an issue is identified.
- Once confirmed, SRE opens the incident ticket.
- The system then generates the group and incident document.
- For critical outages, SRE calls L2 without delay.
- Assigned responsible parties must click to acknowledge the incident.
- After resolution, the team completes the incident report.
- Each Tuesday, the weekly meeting checks incidents still open.
- The large group gets weekly notes on recurring issues and delayed closures.
