- Fault classification leaves out platform display problems, machine hardware faults, and platform prompt optimization.
- Daily tickets or requirement tracking handle those excluded display, hardware, and prompt items.

- On-duty internal-field student submissions are excluded by default.
- Overseas CPU business exists only for Wyneon System-cea8a4ef20 scenarios.
- Wyneon has no GPU business.
