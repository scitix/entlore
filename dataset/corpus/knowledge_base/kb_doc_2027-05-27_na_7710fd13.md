## Core Function Summary

The service offers /ask and /ask_stream as external Q&A interfaces, returning either standard responses or streamed answers. Before retrieval results are used, accessible documents are narrowed by user identity, company entity, department coverage, and document-directory mappings. Retrieval is delegated to the external nexoion_rag service, after which the repository prepares prompts and invokes multiple large-model backends to produce final answers. Pelshaw also includes workflows for preparing knowledge-base content from Confluence and Andromedia, with README notes covering scheduled crawling and ingestion. For offline validation, evaluation scripts call the service in batches from Excel or question sets and use Azure OpenAI to score answer quality.
