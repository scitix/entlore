## Roadmap and Delivery Cadence

Quilholm AI Writing is aligning the 2025H2 plan to the weekly report and briefing core KR, with delivery staged through Luna Ingram. For 2025-H2 KR1, the priority is to make weekly reports, biweekly reports, and briefings reliable, keep usage steady in Luna Ingram, and prepare them for wider promotion. In Q3, reports and biweekly reports are the main vehicles for shaping the product iteration path, using evaluation, metrics, data, and feedback as the loop. The Q3 delivery target is to finish adoption at group level.

| Milestone | Scope | Expected outcome |
|---|---|---|
| M1 | Reporting POC validation | Data interaction works smoothly, the generated draft reaches 70% usable content, and post-editing supports the final output. |
| M2 | Biweekly report expansion | The flow extends to biweekly reports, with key steps improved so the writing outcome is better than reporting. |
| M3 | Evaluation construction | Evaluation sets are built for outline generation, content matching, and final drafts, with measurable results from user feedback and manual review. |

## Q4 Goals

| Milestone | Q4 focus | Delivery target |
|---|---|---|
| Overall | Promotion and coverage | Push biweekly reports across the compute business line, broaden support to weekly reports, and continue solid landing work. |
| M1 | Metric-led optimization | Refine the product further using evaluation metrics and promote biweekly reports across the whole Luna Ingram. |
| M2 | Material collection | Support automatic gathering of personal Feishu documents, meeting minutes, and related materials for personal weekly report writing. |
