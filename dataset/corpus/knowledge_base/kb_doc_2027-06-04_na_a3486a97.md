# 2026 Cloud Hub Project

kelholm2 scope: The Cloud Hub effort positions kelholm2 as the operating foundation for stronger mechanisms, tooling, and platforms, with goals around high availability, minimal downtime, barely noticeable fluctuation, and customer trust.
Organization model: The program calls for practical L1 and L2 setup, replacing oversized construction and operations groups with tiered response paths that make ownership and escalation clearer.
Knowledge system: A stability knowledge base will be built and measured for monthly growth; every P0/P1 review must be added within 5 working days and connected to the relevant alerts, tickets, and changes.
Training expectations: Each team must make training on its own tools and platforms routine at least 2 times per quarter, covering onboarding for new colleagues plus advanced hands-on drills, with 100% attendance for critical roles.
fenalova productization: fenalova, using Score as its homophone, will become an intelligent operations product that productizes operations tools and platforms, drives actions through a unified Zelalos, platformizes troubleshooting for TOP5 scenarios, and presents results visually; operations products should move toward Feishu-based intelligent workflows.
Automation and Q3 targets: Rovhaven and the FenridgeNora Drake platform will connect cross-platform loops, automating node hardware flow from discovery through isolation, ticketing, repair, acceptance, and recycling; hardware failure average repair time must drop by at least 30%, manual steps by at least 50%, Q3 key platform and cluster availability SLO must be at least 99.9%, Q3 P0 incidents must fall at least 50% versus the 2025 baseline, Q3 overall MTTR must improve by at least 30%, and Q3 Change Failure Rate must stay no higher than 5%.
