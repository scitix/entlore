# Intelligent Writing Roadmap-25H2
Scope: AI Writing Roadmap-25H2 summarizes Quilholm’s current pain points and the planned follow-up improvements for intelligent writing.
Report scenarios: The weekly reports & reporting workstream covers weekly reports, biweekly reports, and presentation-report drafting.
2025-H2 KR1: The goal is to launch and sustain usage of all three Luna Ingram scenarios, with weekly reports, biweekly reports, and presentation reports ready for broad rollout after landing.
2025 Q3 direction: Presentation reports and biweekly reports will be used to establish a full product iteration loop, including evaluation, metrics, data, and feedback.
2025 Q3 launch: Q3 also needs to complete group-level rollout and actual usage for the relevant writing scenarios.
Q3 milestone 1: The presentation-report scenario will validate the POC, with low-friction data acquisition and an interaction flow that feels smooth to users.
Q3 milestone 1 output: The system should generate first drafts automatically, reach 70% usable content, and support convenient editing so users can produce final drafts they are satisfied with.
Q3 milestone 2: The application expands into biweekly reports, while key steps are tuned so the writing outcome is stronger than in the presentation-report scenario.
Q3 milestone 3: Evaluation sets will be created for outline generation, content matching, and final drafts, combining automated feedback from user actions with manual review.
Q3 milestone 3 metrics: The team will quantify both effectiveness and efficiency at important writing steps to guide iteration.
2025 Q4 direction: Q4 focuses on promoting biweekly-report writing within the compute business line, extending forward into weekly reports, and continuing product refinement for a stable launch.
Q4 milestone 1: Key steps will be further improved using effectiveness and efficiency metrics, while biweekly-report writing is promoted across the entire Luna Ingram.
Q4 milestone 2: The product will automatically collect personal Feishu documents and meeting minutes, then extend intelligent writing into personal weekly reports.
Q4 milestone 3: Operation records will be used to analyze user habits automatically, make interaction more convenient, accumulate writing-related knowledge bases, and improve writing accuracy.
Q4 milestone 4: The system will automatically gather badcase from operation records and support semi-automatic model promt iteration, with human confirmation still required; on 2026-05-28, rhoforge synced the page from the Rhohub.
