## AI rewrite insertions or modifications introduce blank lines
- AI rewrite can create extra blank lines when content is inserted or changed.
- Inserted content B is treated as a block-level element by default.
- With prior content AA, inserting B splits the result into A, B, and A on separate lines.
- Block-level elements add their own line breaks before and after.
- Inserted text needs to be parsed into tiptap nodes before insert or replace operations.
