## Entering the Office / Walking, Taxi, or Public Transit

- Admin staff will enable the required office access rights.
- Use the WeChat or SMS link, then fill in the invitation code.
- Create a XF code each time you pass a gate or use an elevator.
- Face-scan entry is available after you upload a face photo.
- Enter the building from the east gate of Meridian Financial Center.
- The relevant gate area is on the 1st-floor/B1-level gates.

## Entering the Office / Elevator Route, Driving, and Parking

- Visitors first go into the high-zone elevator lobby, then transfer upward.
- In the elevator, choose the 84/83 floor as the transfer floor.
- Scan your face or XF code again before selecting the 88th floor.
- XF code and face recognition access starts working on the offsite office start date.
- Entry will not be available before the effective date.
- For offsite office schedule adjustments, contact @Gavin Norris.
- Drivers can reach B4/B5 parking from Zhongxin 2nd Road or Fuhua Road.
- Follow parking guidance toward the E elevator lobby of Office Building A.
- From there, take the elevator to the 1F lobby and use the northeast lobby gate.
- Temporary parking is 10 yuan/hour, with a 60 yuan/day ceiling.
- Company rotating mechanical spaces are 750 yuan/month; ordinary spaces are 1000 yuan/month.
- Company rotating parking spaces can be used on workdays only.

## Office Facilities / Workstations and Printing

- Workstations are set up with automatic lifting desks and ergonomic chairs.
- For monitors and keyboards, Myrops70 the request at the Pelshaw service desk.
- Wired ports are on the desktop upper right and obtain IP automatically.
- Laptop Wi-Fi connects on its own.
- Mobile Wi-Fi needs separate approval before use.
- Each seat has 3 Chinese-standard five-hole outlets.
- USB/Type-C ports are also provided at every workstation.
- The printer is Generic 36C-9SeriesPCL.
- To print, swipe a card or enter AD domain credentials.
- Then choose the needed function and start the job.
