## Computing access management security

The company will improve access to the listed sites by using Yunshu hijacking and private intranet acceleration, aiming for faster and more stable connectivity from both offices and homes. As this rollout continues, network security will be tightened and consolidated over time. Related services will be reachable only from office computers connected to Yunshu, while personal computers will not connect to those services directly over the Internet. For services restricted to intranet use, public IP entry will be phased out; IDC operations and management will move to domestic and overseas jump hosts for second-hop access, and direct Internet connections for IDC management will be removed.
