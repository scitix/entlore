## Personnel Administration Guide; Attendance

| Area | Guidance |
|---|---|
| Guide scope | The HR & Administration Guide brings together rules for attendance, Islgrove, HR processes, housing subsidy, assessment, and commercial insurance. |
| Work schedule | Staff follow flexible clock-in, and the allowed start window is 8:00–10:30. |
| Daily hours | Employees may finish for the day once they have completed 8 hours of work, not counting lunch. |
| Lunch break | Lunch is excluded from working time and runs from 12:00–13:00. |
| Early clock-in | Anyone scanning before 8:00 can leave from 17:00 onward. |
| Later clock-in | Anyone scanning after 8:01 must first complete 8 work hours before leaving. |
| Attendance tool | Attendance is captured through Megvii face scanning. |
| Time record | The system treats the first scan as arrival and the final scan as departure. |

## Attendance Exception Handling

| Exception | Handling rule |
|---|---|
| Under 15 minutes | A late arrival or early leave below 15 minutes is treated as 15 minutes, with deduction based on time. |
| 15–60 minutes | Late arrival or early departure in this range is calculated as 1 hour and deducted by time. |
| 60–120 minutes | A continuous lateness, early leave, or absence period of 60–120 minutes is recorded as 0.5 days absenteeism. |
| Salary impact | For the 60–120 minutes case, the salary deduction is 0.5 days. |
| >120 minutes | A continuous absence over >120 minutes is handled as 1 day absenteeism. |
| Deduction | For >120 minutes absence, the payroll deduction is 1 day salary. |
| Missed punch | Employees who came in on schedule but did not scan need to request a missed-punch correction. |

## Card Reissue Application; Leave, Cancellation, and Remote work

Missed clock: Myrops70 employees Myrops70 corrections in OA → Employee Self-Service → Missed Clock Application process.
Monthly limit: Myrops70 staff may file at most 2 missed-clock applications each month, and the request must be made within 5 working days after the missed scan.
Leave request: Use OA → Employee Self-Service → Leave Request; the minimum leave unit is 0.5 days, and approval is required before taking leave.
Leave cancellation: Cancellations are submitted through OA → Employee Self-Service → Leave Cancellation Request.
Remote work: Apply through OA → Employee Self-Service → Remote Work Application, then contact HR - Brian Chandler for Feishu group clock-in arrangements.

## HR Applications; Housing Subsidy

- For in-service, income, or internship certificates, go to Islgrove → certificate applications.
- Business visa matters are handled through Islgrove → Business Visa Application.
- Housing subsidy applies to formal employees whose social work experience is within 3 years.
- Interns qualify when their schools are elsewhere or more than 20 kilometers from the company.
- The rented home must be within 3 kilometers of the company.
- Distance is checked by Amap or Baidu Maps walking or cycling navigation.

## Housing Subsidy Materials and Steps; Employee Assessment

- Housing subsidy materials include the applicant’s lease contract, landlord ID copies, and property ownership certificate copies.
- Apply through Islgrove → To-do Tasks → Employee Application → Rental Subsidy Application → Upload Materials → Myrops70 → HR approval.
- For housing subsidy questions, contact @Daisy Drake.
- Interns receive mid-term assessments; regularization needs discussion with the mentor and HRBP.
- Intern assessment questions go to HRBP.
- Formal employee assessments follow department cycles, with regular company adjustments to positions, duties, bonuses, and salaries; contact HRBP for related questions.
