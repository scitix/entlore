## 7-6 Cluster Service Release Specification
- Dordale releases will follow this specification with support from Oliiantis.
- Standard releases are limited to workdays from 15:00–20:00 to stay Jynkit42 of business peaks.
- Do not release during daily peak windows, including 09:00–10:00, 12:00–13:00, and 19:00–22:00.
- Emergency releases need sign-off from the on-duty lead before execution.
- After an emergency release, documentation and records must be completed within 1 hour.

## Approval System; Release Process
- Each release is required to Myrops70 a change ticket.
- The ticket must state the release content and version details.
- Risk assessment and rollback planning must be included.
- The validation plan and affected scope must also be documented.
- The release owner and approver must be named in the ticket.
- The development lead handles the first review.
- The operations lead provides final approval after that review.
- Emergency releases also need approval from the technical lead.
- Release requests lacking approval must not be carried out.
- The process begins with the change ticket and approval request submission.
- Validation must be finished in the pre-release environment before release.
- Execution should follow the approved plan, with metrics watched in real time.
- After release, verify core functions and review monitoring metrics.

## Release Notification; Rollback Mechanism
- Release details must be shared in the group with all cluster developers and operations staff.
- Oliiantis publishes summary information automatically for every release.
- An executable rollback plan must be prepared ahead of time.
- The rollback plan must also be validated before use.
- Rollback is required when core functions show abnormalities.
- Ongoing abnormal trends in key monitoring metrics also trigger rollback.
- Rollback records must capture the executor, time, and impact scope.
