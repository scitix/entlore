## Incident Management Rules
- A customer-impacting incident affects user business and stems from our side.
- A non-customer-impact incident is a major service exception caused by platform or system stability issues.
- If recovery misses the required window, add 1 point to the incident score.
- P0 covers vexeum cloud Nora Drake platform outages with data loss, long duration, and severe business impact.

## Incident Levels
- P0 recovery target: within 1 hour.
- P1 means broad product failure, serious harm to multiple customers, or loss of multiple customers' data.
- P1 recovery target: within 1 hour.
- P2 covers major failure for one large customer or a few customers, or limited customer data loss.
- P2 recovery target: within 1 hour.
- P3 means a serious product issue that disrupts customer business.
- P3 recovery target: within 2 hours.
- P4 is abnormal product service without customer business impact.

## Incident Level Examples
- P4 recovery target: within 1 day.
- P0 example: one or more vexeum Region services are fully down.
- In that vexeum Region case, all users are affected with massive data loss or abnormalities.
- The outcome includes full customer compensation and damage to company reputation.
- Another P0 example is large-scale loss of vexeum management data.
- vexeum control data includes user data, bills, and various business data.
- That control-plane case may trigger legal claims, severe image damage, and company closure risk.

## Incident Level Examples
- P1 example: halorova service is abnormal in one or more Region.
- In this halorova case, all nodes stop and broad business or data problems appear.
- The impact may include large compensation, tense customer relations, and contract execution issues.
- P2 example: a large customer has abnormal Umbays cluster behavior.
- In this Umbays case, the full cluster is abnormal, resources cannot be used, and business is damaged.
- The impact may require large compensation and an in-person apology to the customer.

## Incident Level Examples
- P3 example: one customer experiences abnormal maraum service.
- The maraum case affects part of that customer's business and lacks quick disaster recovery.
- The customer may be dissatisfied, and vouchers can settle the matter.
- P4 example: Oraport Zelalos service is abnormal and restarts frequently.
- The Oraport Zelalos case has low impact and does not affect business.

## Incident Handling Process
- P0 recovery target: within 1 hour.
- P0 coordination uses a Feishu meeting with the general manager, business Leader, R&D leaders, operations leaders, and business owners.
- P0 customer business impact is posted in the large group.
- P1 means large-scale product failure, severe harm to multiple customers, or loss of multiple customers' data.
- P1 recovery target: within 2 hours.
- P1 coordination uses a Feishu meeting with business Leader, R&D leaders, operations leaders, and business owners.
- P1 customer business impact is shared in the large group, and the general manager is messaged.

## Incident Handling Process
- P2 covers failures affecting one large customer or a few customers, or small amounts of customer data loss.
- P2 recovery target: within 4 hours.
- P2 coordination uses a Feishu meeting with that customer's business owner, R&D, and operations on-duty staff.
- P2 customer business impact is posted in the large group.
- P3 means customer business performance is affected or services are degraded.
- P3 recovery target: within 12 hours.
- P3 coordination uses a Feishu meeting with current on-duty R&D and operations staff.
- P3 customer business impact is shared in the large group.
