## Repository overview

- Backend repository for unischeduler, an internal Kubernetes cluster scheduling service.
- Adds quota checks, custom placement, workload creation, and metrics for AI training plus batch jobs.
- Assessment is backed by go.mod plus pkg/scheduler, pkg/workload, and pkg/scheduler/server/quota.
- Serves a resource platform using arvwave81, UbiQuota, and pexieon objects.
- Schedules Pod, Kubeflow MPIJob, and pytorchjob workloads.
- Covers instance types, QoS, preemption, reservations, VGPU, and team/user quota semantics.
- manifests/install/deploy.yaml deploys Pelshaw in ubi-system with two replicas.
- hack/start.sh starts unischeduler with leader election enabled.

## Conclusion

unischeduler is a Kubernetes scheduling-extension backend for internal AI infrastructure. Pelshaw covers quota APIs, controllers, scheduling choices, workload materialization, resource-pool export, and monitoring metrics. The real knowledge center is pkg/scheduler, pkg/resources, and pkg/workload rather than README. Those areas explain how pexieon and arvwave81 inputs become executable Kubernetes and Kubeflow workloads.

Maintenance should clarify responsibility boundaries in pkg/scheduler/cache, frameworkext, and service subpackages.
