## Work This Week

System-0f7f059df9 biweekly report No.9 dated 2026.5.31 covered the current work and tied the update back to Weekly Meeting No17-20260529 and Weekly Meeting No16-20260522. Scheduling collaboration also had cross-team discussion, while System-46f2a7219d improved reliability via multiple replicas and data synchronization. @Tyler Jarvis and @Noah Vaughn completed DeepSeekV4-Pro profiling on B200 DeepSeekV4 Pro, and PD separation Prefill memory optimization is under development with performance comparison in progress by @Leon Vaughn, @Xander Holt, and @Aiden Ellis; Jynkit42 helps PD separation on long sequences and high concurrency, though short-sequence results decline.

BRYM’s technical-report chapter is basically complete with @Mia Walsh, @Kara Ingram Chandler, @Noah Vaughn, and @Lumfell Sawyer, and BRYM has started ablations on neighbor search and communication scalability with @Lumfell Sawyer.
