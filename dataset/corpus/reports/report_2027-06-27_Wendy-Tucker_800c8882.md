## This Week's Work

System-0f7f059df9 biweekly report No.10 dated 2026.6.14 captured the week’s progress, with supporting detail in System-a524add015 and System-c8b95b22f7. DALOROVA finished the 6.15 lororys launch, while @Leon Mercer closed the lororys inference optimization goal and follow-up postmortem work; the same stream kept pace with GLM-5.2 and the July iteration, focusing on FP4, added parallelism, and broader optimization.
