This week, the team lifted overall PDF parsing capacity from 1.5 ten-thousand documents/day to 15 ten-thousand documents/day. We improved render parallelism, streaming submission, batch alignment, and asynchronous link blocking fixes, which materially raised parsing efficiency and throughput.
