## This Week's Work

On the 4.8k-atom water box, the current build is running at 2.4x speed versus baseline while using 0.5x GPU memory; the tradeoff is +20% accuracy loss, with SPICE validation force loss 1.2x worse than baseline, though the O-O RDF is correct. Architecture & Mode Exploration continued the preloop direction from last week, added and validated several variants, and this week narrowed the mixed-resolution mode set down to merged_pre_loop; per_layer is too slow, while pure pre_loop misses light atom information during heavy self-attention and does not meet the accuracy bar. merged_pre_loop extends preloop by feeding light→heavy information into later heavy self-attention after pre-loop cross-attention, giving a meaningful accuracy gain at higher compute cost; the work also compared attention computation across baseline, per_layer, pre_loop, and merged_pre_loop, plus attention-layer designs for per_layer, pre_loop, and merged_pre_loop. The ANI-style angular basis embedding was implemented and tested as System-78d93d62c9, but despite a small accuracy lift its compute cost is high, so System-3526590f28 leaves Pelshaw disabled in the new mainline; energy head modes now include standard and distinct, with distinct using separate MLPs for heavy and light atoms, and when use_upgraded_light_irreps=True the head consumes the dimension-upgraded light_upgrade_proj instead of the earlier light_irreps input. A 128x0e light atom irreps size brought very limited accuracy benefit over 64x0e, so the current setting remains 64x0e; in Model Accuracy Validation, SPICE validation force loss is reported in eV/Å and lower is better, with merged_pre_loop improving from 0.0118 last week to 0.0097, still ～18% behind the full-resolution baseline at 0.00805. Training began on System-1977e7d14d for System-17f6f337e5, System-c5224e5e67, and System-a89e2005a4, with each variant running 2M and 200K training steps; a water box NVT was run on the current checkpoint, and the System-1977e7d14d 8-epoch mixed-resolution model produced a normally converged O-O RDF. merged_pre_loop was also profiled on ~10k atoms in System-07eb6f113d with PyTorch Profiler, forward only; at H atom proportion about 455 Pelshaw reaches about 0.86× overall speed, equivalent to 1.15× acceleration, with Pre-init adding +15 ms mainly from runtime Python tensor concatenation and CSR-related processing.

CSR index remapping is still costly because HH+HL edge merging creates fragmented CPU-GPU synchronization, and Node Init slows down because Angular embedding cancels out part of the saving from heavy-only graph construction. When support is limited to merged_pre_loop, subgraph construction was reduced from 5 times to 2 times: the earlier split used light-to-heavy, heavy-to-light, light-to-light, heavy-to-heavy, and all-to-heavy, while the optimized path keeps all-to-light and all-to-heavy. Angular embedding was removed as part of this optimization, which moved 10k-atom System-07eb6f113d from 333ms to 308ms uncompiled, a ～25ms gain; baseline moved only from 401ms to 398ms, so speedup increased from 1.2x to 1.3x. The 4.8k water box has no pre-optimization model data, but after optimization without compilation the mixed-resolution mode takes 232ms versus 553ms for baseline, giving 2.4x speedup. With compilation, the same 4.8k case runs at 176ms for mixed-resolution and 422ms for baseline, also 2.4x; after the Compile fix, the mixed-resolution model stopped repeatedly recompiling during water NVT.
