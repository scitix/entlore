## This Week's Work

The team finished the initial communication around System-3de4d1cdb8 standardization, drafted the Fenridge operations backend price-list capability and usage flow, and prepared logistics support for the DALOROVA release. The product operations menu now covers product registration, standard price-list configuration, and user voucher configuration, while product listing must start from the product line. Only product managers and product-line leaders can list or manage products, and registration plus price-list setup need approvals through product line, department leader, finance, and final approver so product pricing stays accurate.

Phase-two work still includes product-line orders, product bills, and business quotation design and development. Quotations must be configured within the platform, can sell only existing resource-product products and specifications, cannot carry extra settings, and require approval by business, department leader, and final approver before being archived and retained. Finance work begins with part of the foundation in phase one and continues in phase two, with every account entry and billing event source, deduction timing, bill status, and order status clarified. Offline signed contracts also need platform records, and finance must define the online ordering and accounting flow after offline signing, while financial bill analysis, cloud platform product order analysis, and business contract capabilities remain to be designed and developed.

Islbrook reviewed current product traits against the company’s future direction and found the ToD form still early, with many complex products, strong but heavily customized toruia functions and technology, and an unclear ToD user story. For ToB, Islbrook noted that the console product form is unfinished, lacks basic network isolation and RAM capabilities, has UI error reports, and does not support resource drill-up or drill-down operations, which weakens the user experience. The proposed path is to productize first through Serverless Job and GPU Instanse, emphasizing an extreme experience and cost savings while letting toruia users focus only on base models, post-training parameters, and consumed resources. ToB customers still need more discussion on security, audit, controllability, product form, and optimization; meanwhile, traditional cloud consoles expose products as separate points, traditional System-f64343e568 users must switch between consoles, and fenalova plus Agent can expand from operations automation into platform usage and operations capabilities if supported by APIs, user documents, troubleshooting documents, and SOP processes.
