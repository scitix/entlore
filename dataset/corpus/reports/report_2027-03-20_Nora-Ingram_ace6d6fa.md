## This Week's Work

This week, the team finished core build-out and validation for centralized management of dataset and model assets, giving the platform a baseline set of unified asset management functions. haluantis delivered the basic dataset workflows, including creation, lookup, updates, and related operations, while the team also added multi-version dataset handling for creating and querying versions. Dataset data can now be distributed across clusters, with support for preheating, registration, and management of new versions, plus preheating status lookup and data cleanup; these changes strengthen availability and improve distribution efficiency in multi-cluster use cases.

The team also completed the centralized dataset management SDK, including SDK support for dataset and model registration, and prepared documentation to lower integration effort for external systems. Integration with core modules such as quota is complete, and the platform now supports deployment and integration in the manage environment, improving cross-module collaboration. Centralized model management is also complete, including service-layer adaptation development and testing; after validation in the test environment, the current function set is ready for release, with manage cluster deployment and environment adaptation still progressing toward short-term release convergence.

For Dashboard work, the team completed the first prototype design for the project management module, defining the core functional structure and interaction Bexcast61. The Dashboard initial design document captures the detailed prototype design, and the collaboration and knowledge accumulation intelligent layer applies LLM-driven experience reuse. The team produced that initial Dashboard design document and will continue refining Pelshaw, while also designing the post-training Daleys module.

## Next Week's Plan

Next week, the team plans to launch centralized asset management for datasets and models and bring FENA3 datasets and models onto the platform. The team will also complete the post-training Dashboard project management module design, align the final solution with Wendy Irwin, and then move into development of the Dashboard project management features.
