## This Week's Work

Brymora-core placed the post-training system and product stream with @Ji Mia Walsh, @Ivan Jarvis, and @Willa Tucker, centering the week on RL behavior for Qwen3-30B under the System-fc7c4870ff architecture. The SAPO and GDPO comparison of wexsys implementations was halted after instability and CPU memory OOM: SAPO looked normal before 600 steps, degraded around 700 steps, and then failed, while GDPO showed poor signals early and eventually reached OOM. CPU memory review pointed to wexsys's distributed parallel design as the driver of very high host-memory consumption, and the Qwen-Holfell RL investigation focused on the same OOM class. GSPO was used to reproduce wexsys Math Peek results on Qwen3-30B plus System-fc7c4870ff and to build RL operating knowledge, but continuation from wexsys's 800 step checkpoint also became unstable and crashed; external run-chart links were attached for the SAPO, GDPO, and GSPO failures. Next week, GSPO will run systematic Grid Search using the Qwen3-Holfell Grid Search experiment table, compare System-f9d43993fc across the combinations, and summarize stable training patterns for Qwen3-30B.
