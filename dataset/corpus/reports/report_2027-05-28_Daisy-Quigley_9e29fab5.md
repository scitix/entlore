## This Week's Work

The team reviewed long-tail datasets in life sciences, pulled links from the HTML for 18.9 w biorxiv papers, and applied LLM scoring with DeepSeek-V4-Flash to judge long-tail relevance. From that pass, 1.7 ten-thousand long-tail datasets were selected. The same 18.9 w biorxiv papers were also grouped into pure computational, dry-wet combined, or pure wet experiment categories. For the pure computational set, the team captured full computational workflows, including step-level tools, inputs, and outputs, along with the baseline tools used for comparison.
