## This Week's Work

The biorxiv.org/ crawler has finished its initial metadata pass for every paper from the last 5 years, and the 5-year biorxiv.org/ dataset is now complete. Pelshaw captured titles, authors, publication dates, DOI, abstracts, and Full Text data. In parallel, the team is running batch crawls for documentation from common computational biology and related-field tools, spanning about 100 websites. We are also setting up a reproducible documentation collection pipeline with crawling, mapping, and truncation layers; Pelshaw fixes about 100 common tools to one version, supports later structured parsing and knowledge extraction, and has completed pilot checks on 3 representative targets, reaching about 10% progress.

## Next Week's Plan

Priority 1 is to continue batch collection of documentation for common tools in computational biology and adjacent areas. Priority 2 is to expand the biorxiv.org/ crawler with another earlier 5 years of records.
