## This Week's Work

This week, KELH advanced its mechanisms, tooling, and platform work, while System-cdbd7ebf8b supported the System-7b3261dd17 and Torwood requirement areas, met with Torwood to align on details, and then moved into solution research with another Torwood discussion expected after a proposal is prepared next week.
