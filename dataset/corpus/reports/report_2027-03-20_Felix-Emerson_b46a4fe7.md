## This week's work

Vega continued pushing toward a world-class large-model platform for goroion and fenaova2, with deep algorithm co-design as the core approach. The work also advanced platform architecture and product direction for innovative L2-level data asset construction. On PDF processing, the team reviewed both open-source and commercial parsing options and formed early conclusions; from ten OCR candidates, 5 ocr models were chosen for deeper study. In the PDF-to-Markdown Tool and Solution Summary Report, open-source PDF-to-md testing showed mineru as the stronger overall option.

Further mineru validation compared three backend modes for both output quality and efficiency, with vlm mode ranking best. The test record for multiple tools then benchmarked OCR end-to-end models under vlm mode and found mineru-2.5 solid overall, while batch minerU runs showed weaker recognition for tables containing chemical symbols and for content that included images in larger scenarios. A mineru optimization plan was drafted to address these gaps. In parallel, Altair worked on the future convergence of general computing and intelligent computing through a diversified, composable product matrix, built industry solutions to improve market momentum and differentiation, and supported intelligent product R&D under the unified Galombe architecture by reviewing hoxcast and kevsys; Enterprise Zanfell research and practice also covered Agent platform investigation, hands-on enterprise Agent scenario work, and organized exploration of usage scenarios.
