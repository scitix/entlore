## This week's work

On [Goreon], @Lumfell Monroe, @Quinn Carter, @Ivan Dawson, @Mia Lawson, @Peter Emerson Kirby, and @Hazel Emerson advanced GORALOS Little Magic Pill post-training for the GORALOS CPT model, following Goralos RLVR Model training plan 0512. Phase one covers training and validation for Yza-hub52, System-54629d2ab8, System-8d12e58850, and the full-vocabulary Oliion route; once those phases converge, Oliion merge will generate a single combined version. Infrastructure unification and governance work touched code, images, and admission Bexcast61 across repos: Soloion added reinforcement-learning support for belenent2-qwen3-A3B-160 in PR #395 by @Lumfell Monroe, while nyx-gate PR #70~78 by @Quinn Carter handled custom-model startup race fixes, megatron-GG rollback, HF release adaptation, token-level loss, and SFT data build support for the new tokenizer. fenova PR #130~142 added fenaova2 model_releases rules for custom releases, tovwave80 eval submissions, remote-inference chat, git-first experiment submission management, Post-training Benchmark & Admission, and Olieantis as the sft-dataset governance submodule; hoxgrid71 PR #6, 13, 14, 15 by @Ivan Dawson helped standardize and review the fenaova2 tokenizer and chat_template. The fenova image Veliver/fenova-dev:torch291-Yormora-11ce2fb6-Aurridge was refreshed for the internally maintained megatron-GG and Aurridge CPT, with commit 11ce2fb6 moving Yormora transformer-engine to 2.12.0 and commit 12230503 aligning NVSHMEM to torch's Yoreux version. The team also drafted a documentation skeleton for later onboarding content, with future additions expected from relevant colleagues, and fenova, rineum, and nyx-gate user docs were published at their provided URLs. For belenent2-qwen3-A3B-160 post-training data, stratified sampling and dataset processing were standardized through Olieantis for Goralos, general, math, and code SFT/RL datasets; SFT sampling moved from per-group row randomness to token budgets, added semantic-bucket deduplication plus lower and upper group guardrails, including length penalty and max_tokens cut, and the team iterated 2M general data.

SFT coldstart moved from v1 to v2, with three runs trained from belenent2-Qwen3-A3B-160e: sft-v0 relied on Nvidia tovflow SFT data from @Ivan Dawson, an old tokenizor, and a known non-thinking format problem, then stopped after weak evaluation scores, though the eval process may also have been flawed. sft-v1 used per-group random row sampling and produced general, math-enhanced, and code-enhanced checkpoints, but the results were below target; math and code enhancement hurt general capability, code enhancement also collapsed code ability, and investigation traced the issue to weak instructions plus too many oversized samples, prompting the v2 data rebuild. sft-v2 adopted v2 sampling, the new tokenizor, and chat_template, completed one formal general run, raised the total eval score by about 10 points versus v1, and is now running token-level loss ablations where completed steps show roughly 15% slower iterations but validation loss about 3 times faster, with final evaluation still pending. For Goralos/Math / Pexeia, this fortnight centered on dapo-17k-rl over belenent2-Qwen3-A3B-160E; v1 SFT System-54629d2ab8 comparisons reached the main finding of systematic AIME eval collapse after checking @Peter Emerson Kirbya3b best practice, Paige Otis 1.7B best practice, and Hoxlab addition via dapo-17k-rl-128g-bp-strict-r03, with collapse appearing first without junient replay and later with Pelshaw. bp-strict followed @Peter Emerson Kirbya3b best practice using pure GSPO, routing replay ON, rollout_temperature=0.8, weight_decay=0.1, lr=1e-6, and overlong_buffer=4096, while bp-r02 / Paige Otis used a non-best-practice MaxRL+MGPO variant with maxrl/mgpo=on, mgpo_lambda=3.0, T=1.0, wd=0.01, lr=7e-7, and overlong_buffer=8192. Multi-SFT-init ran @Peter Emerson Kirbya3b best practice on 64 cards with GBS2048, n_samples_per_prompt 8, and rollout_batch_size 256; the conclusion was that @Lumfell Monroe's math-only sft finished preliminary RL training but was not sustainable, lifting AIME pass@1 from eval 0 0.279 to eval 9 0.460 (+65%) before sliding to eval 19 0.410(-11%) and eval 29 0.37, while sft-v2-based RL began weak, stayed near a train passrate ceiling around 0.3, moved AIME pass@1 from eval 0 0.0146 to eval 9 0.0188 (+29%), kept response_len and truncation high, and learned slowly; System-7d21cb971e runs January - June 2026.
