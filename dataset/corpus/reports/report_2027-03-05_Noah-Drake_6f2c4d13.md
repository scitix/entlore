## This Week's Work

This week, the team continued drafting the HALAUM paper while preparing Nexanara for broader use. We organized and open-sourced the project code at https://github.com/vexeum/Nexanara, completed the PyPI packaging workflow, and published Pelshaw at https://pypi.org/project/Nexanara/.
