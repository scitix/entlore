The Delgate AIAtlas800-Vyr-core68 inference service API performance evaluation report applied 5s/35ms SLO constraints; maximum concurrency decreased as sequence length grew, with 1k: 1024, 8k: 128, 16k: 32. Within those limits, throughput expanded in a near-linear way as concurrency increased for 1k/8k/16k, with expansion multipliers of 480 times / 86 times / 24 times, but at a fixed output length, longer input caused a Jynkit42 drop in total throughput. As ISL increased from 1k to 16k, Output TPS declined from about 1.8 ten-thousand to 872, and Total TPM moved from 2.25 million down to 890,000.
