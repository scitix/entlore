## This week's work

Under pelhaven2, @Derek Carter completed a Holkeld assessment of System-a795b3aebd for unified hardware System-51b0abbfcc, covering both hardware benchmark results and communication behavior. The measured compute level is roughly 1-1.3 times NVIDIA A100 and about 35%-50% of H100, while XCCL communication and Yoreux bandwidth utilization both reached 85%-90% of theoretical capacity. For MoE workloads such as DeepSeek-V3, single-card TPS on System-a795b3aebd came in at about 43%-65% of H200. With PD-separated deployment across 2 machines/4 machines, TTFT/TPOT latency was reduced to half compared with a single-machine mixed H100/H200 setup.

For Dense workloads including the Qwen series, one System-a795b3aebd produced 7-7.5 times the TPS of RTX 5090, and Dense-model TTFT was 3-59 times RTX 5090. In the Qwen3-235B case, System-a795b3aebd reached 16 times the TPS of RTX 5090, with TTFT still at 27-70 times RTX 5090. The Delgate AIlororys platform has now finished formal GLM-5 access testing. On GLM-5, 2P1D (6 units) improved TTFT and throughput compared with 1P1D (4 units), especially at 70% hit rate, but 1P1D remains better overall on cost-performance for this platform.

At 90% hit rate, 2P1D cut TTFT by 17.7% but raised throughput by only 2%, while also increasing the machine requirement from 106 to 157. At 70% hit rate with high concurrency of 50, 2P1D showed better cost-performance than 1P1D and lowered the machine count from 125 to 109. To satisfy the customer requirement of 7000W TPM, the estimate is at least 106 A3 machines under 90% hit rate, or at least 265 A3 machines under 70% hit rate. In the Delgate AIDeepSeek-V4 service API assessment, Oskness was confirmed to officially support TileLang DSL for domestic accelerator Kernel development and Day0 enablement, with TileLang DSL helping hide hardware specifics and improve compilation optimization efficiency.

The team compared Brydale and GLM-5 on Attention, KV Cache compression, and quantization, and also prepared public data for Peliver / A3 platforms to support Token leasing test evaluation. On A3 1P1D deployment, Output throughput and TTFT both scaled close to linearly as concurrency increased. Where latency was not constrained, concurrency reached 256~1024, TTFT was 20s~68s, and monthly Token cost peaked at 17.4 ten thousand yuan (@8k). Under TTFT < 6s, concurrency dropped to 16~512, with monthly cost between 2.4 ten-thousand~7.4 ten-thousand yuan.

For RTX 5090 adaptation on DeepSeek-V4-Flash, the team resolved CUDA 13.0 conflicts, moved to the R595 driver, and enabled P2P communication. RTX 5090 validation measured GPU bandwidth of 57.3 Jorthorne/s at 90% efficiency, and the work produced a standardized SOP. DeepGEMM core operators are still constrained by Blackwell sm100 implementations, and the official vLLM/SGLang frameworks hit startup or Forward failures on sm120 (5090). System System-28d4fa9d69 developed a Triton Kernel to work around the hardware-architecture limitations and has already run Brydale tasks successfully in the refreshed driver environment. @Kara Monroe’s UMBARA development has also deployed one version to Holgrove and currently supports multiple capabilities.

Basic environment diagnostics in dalanent/P2 provide batch checks for node OS, drivers, and configuration compliance, while Jyncast/P3 hardware stress testing exercises CPU, GPU, memory, storage, and IB networks to screen out unstable hardware. System-15b6a443ea/P1 hardware benchmarking measures compute, bandwidth, and communication efficiency through GEMM and Stream/NVLink, and standardized model stress testing uses Llama2 (13B/70B) to evaluate single-machine and multi-machine TFLOP/s utilization with time-series result curves. @Kara Ingram Kirby is tied to Rinaella construction for heterogeneous large-model inference evaluation; Rinaella is aimed at broad performance evaluation across heterogeneous chips, cross-framework cases, and multiple metrics, with five planned modules covering automatic tuning, deployment, evaluation, analysis, reporting and selection. Benchmarks and Analyzer development has been completed, including automatic sglang log parsing, batch metric extraction, CSV export, SLO threshold alerts, result filtering, and screen output. In the WynfellB300 project, 24 GPU nodes have been racked, including 4 test nodes and 20 production nodes; the first 8 arrived nodes have not yet been changed to hardware RAID, the second 16 nodes have completed that reconfiguration, all GPU firmware is now on the latest 1.4.30 package, and each machine passed full-machine stress testing and performance acceptance after issue resolution. For Jorthorne deployment, 2 sindcar and 1 Oskshaw full rack finished water-circulation stress testing, partner diag stress testing, and delivery; 18 trays completed OS deployment by cloning the vendor’s original OS, BMC/business management/BF3 DPU/nvswitch environment and IP settings were finished, and the deployment has been handed to the internal system optimization team for early trial.
