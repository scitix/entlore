## This week's work

Rigel moved the unified architecture forward by combining Pelford, xalfield2, and lororys capabilities and resources across the stack, while also shaping common R&D and governance for both internal and external resources. Under the latest architecture, Rigel is aimed at improving R&D and operations efficiency, and the lororys platform pooling now covers lororys2&loraeon; on loraeon, the backend issue was fixed so previously streamed text is saved to the database when a user interrupts a conversation. Vega continued deep algorithm co-design for the world-leading large-model targets goroion and FENA3, while also driving architecture progress, product evolution, and innovation.

For L2 data assets, the team reviewed open-source and paid PDF parsing tools, including 5 mainstream open-source OCR models. The early assessment is that MinerU is the most stable open-source choice, while paddle-ocr-vl, maker, and glm-ocr are also usable and should be paired with other approaches; all open-source paths still need semantic correction and rule-based cleanup. On the paid side, mathfix showed the best results with fewer PDF parsing mistakes, can turn chemical bond images into molecular formulas, and can produce typical labeled data for checking open-source accuracy. Altair focused on the coming convergence of general computing and intelligent computing by building a diversified, composable product matrix and industry solutions, strengthening market traction and differentiated competitiveness. For the unified Agent system, session persistence was updated, automatic sleep during human-machine waiting periods was fixed, and the team reviewed the full technical architecture with @Rachel Zimmer based on the planned agent architecture system, with the overall framework preliminarily determined.
