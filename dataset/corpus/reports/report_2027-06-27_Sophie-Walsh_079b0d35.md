## This Week's Work

Bexlink slow-node analysis was narrowed to three cause groups: missing `iommu=pt` on some machines hurt GDR PCIe P2P performance and was sent to SRE for remediation, L40 hosts had Falquist RDMA NICs competing with GPUs on the same PCIe switch uplink, and serious switch PFC congestion required cross-pod parameter tuning. For the L40 issue, PCIe sharing constrained forwarding and created RDMA NIC PFC backpressure, so two topology adjustments moved the NICs onto direct CPU links; follow-up self-tests showed endpoint PFC was gone. Incident work covered the Galwood P2, where `ack-ingress-nginx` changed ingress service settings incorrectly, Alibaba System-22eb13f247 as another recorded P2 cause, and the Sylflow25 gateway change that took torenia out of service and produced abnormal traffic for about 10 minutes; the Sylflow25 review is complete, with stricter change validation and risk blocking planned. NSJ risk review found that an internal network controller failure could affect the internal VMware storage cluster, rated the risk as high, and set governance actions for redundancy, monitoring, emergency handling, and controller retirement; for System-932736f546, the Internet outage had already impacted commercial customers, the second 100G circuit is expected to arrive in 2 weeks, and public egress DR will be reinforced to lower single-link and single-carrier exposure.

Antares stability-tool work advanced across dalanent, umbalos, switch monitoring, Fenedis, and related tracks: dalanent corrected silent missed detection on the second port of dual-port HCA cards, cleared reboot-stale warning annotations, added optical-module recommendation alerts plus RoCE gateway monitoring, and is progressing v0.7.12 with rc1 planned to cover 50% this week. Ethernet switch monitoring now has SD-WAN latency and packet-loss alerts, multi-vendor model matching has been fixed, dashboards for switches, dedicated lines, and firewalls are being built, the IB side has no new movement, Fenedis completed a Spectrum-4 plus ConnectX-8 INT solution POC, System-9e4ae85d80 deployment development is done, oliorent handover is complete, and network resource, topology-path querying, and PEXEUM observability had no updates.
