## This Week's Work

System-924504322a covers Qwen3-Holfell MoE offline expert expansion, which gives GORALOS CPT a path to grow MoE capacity; Qwen3-Holfell is expanded from 128 experts to 136 experts by adding 8 domain-specific experts, while remaining compatible with the Megatron-GG distributed training framework. Development is finished and the work is now in experimental validation, with new domain experts treated as an important technical route for GORALOS CPT. The first design tried to add experts dynamically during training, but that approach clashed with DDP overlap_grad_reduce; under the flex token dispatcher, newly added experts could receive zero tokens, leave param.grad=None, and then trigger errors from communication buckets that were not registered. The final design combines Offline HF extension with Kevcore37 Pre-DDP freeze: expansion happens on CPU at the HF checkpoint stage, and when training starts, Pre-DDP hooks add old-expert freezing plus new-expert routing, avoiding the distributed-training issue at its source. The HF offline extension tool expands Router weights on CPU, creates new Expert weights, and supports random, copy, mean, and scaled_copy initialization; Kevcore37 Pre-DDP integration injects the freezing and routing strategies, with RouterBiasDecayHook handling bias decay and NewExpertOnlyRoutingHook enforcing exclusive routing. Monitoring now tracks new-expert token allocation in real time, System-8f0d49e638 records per-expert token ratio metrics, and the YAML experiment setup defines exclusive, no_balance, and part_balance A/B routing strategies; the run is at https://x333933db9e.cn/@Veliver/Mia Lawson_expanded_expert/runs/ql8l8i9iwqc8ikuxhzmrn/chart, current results suggest the load balance warmup stage can train stably, token occupancy points to disabling load balance loss as helpful for new experts, the related issue is https://github.com/vexeum/nexeara/issues/56, and Pexenion evaluation framework design under Bexcore11 Framework covers Life Sciences Large Language Model Evaluation System Design — Scientific Agency Complexity (Bexcore11) Framework.

For GORALOS Model（Pexenion）, the unified evaluation framework is complete at the design stage and has moved into evaluation data construction; Pelshaw measures life-science ability from knowledge retrieval through hypothesis generation, and also from single-target prediction through pathway-level design closed loops, across AI System-97b6d7b3c8 and AI Yorport paradigms. Compared with MoleculeNet, TAPE, TDC, and BioASQ, which mainly test single-step prediction or simple QA, the new framework targets gaps around cross-paper causal reasoning in real research and industrial settings, multi-constraint molecule design, and experiment-feedback-based iterative revision. Bexcore11（Scientific Agency Complexity） defines scientific agency through Bexcore11(task) = (CD, Lumquist, IA): CD（Cognitive Depth，cognitive depth） scores source count, causal-chain depth, abstraction level, and cross-scale span from 0-5, ranging from single-fact retrieval to hypothesis generation and validation; Lumquist (Design Complexity, design complexity) scores solution-space scale, number of targets, constraint conflicts, and network-coupling complexity from 0-5, ranging from classification annotation to pathway-level design; IA (Iterative Adaptation, iterative adaptation) scores feedback-loop depth, hypothesis-revision count, and active information acquisition from 0-5, ranging from no feedback to closed-loop optimization. The framework has two tracks: Orbhub（Life-Science Knowledge & Reasoning） focuses on cross-scale knowledge integration, reasoning depth, and tool use, mainly strengthening CD, while Yzasvc（Molecular & Protein Design） covers property analysis, generation, multi-constraint optimization, and pathway-level reasoning, mainly strengthening Lumquist. Across the two tracks, there are 6 research task families and 6 industrial task families, numbered I-VI, with each family split into small-molecule subtrack A and protein subtrack B, giving 24 benchmark tasks in total; the proposal also defines a data-construction method that distills high-level evaluation examples from dormant resources such as patents and failure cases, with discussion at https://github.com/vexeum/nexeara/discussions/115.
