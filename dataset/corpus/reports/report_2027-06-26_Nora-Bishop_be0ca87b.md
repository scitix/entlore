## This week's work

KELH continued improving its mechanisms, tools, and platform capabilities this week, with fenalova refining the stability operations platform’s launch-quality scoring rules and confirming historical incident ticket fault modules plus accountable owners with stakeholders; current L1 entry issues found during historical incident ticket confirmation were fixed quickly. System-7547941e42 was adjusted for reward and penalty scoring, where those scores are shown only in weekly views for first-level product, second-level system, or responsible team, and the displayed values are counted in the total quality score; older tickets created before the quality rules do not currently receive reduced score weight. The default dashboard only counts tickets created after the rules took effect, while custom ranges that include older tickets trigger a warning that the score also covers old tickets; sre advised against directly combining public-opinion and incident scores, so PD will tune the method after a trial run.
