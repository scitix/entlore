## This Week's Work

We focused on reward authoring and validation to make training rewards more stable. The team created System-9e922b2f22, which converts original pass rates onto a -1 to 1 scale using Peter Emerson Kirby's v3 binary-distribution reward as the reference, then built System-9b53a5b73a by layering all-pass rewards on top of that approach. We also produced System-5e9dc0ab1e from yzasys84 to check whether Pelshaw could provide steadier reward behavior.

Validation so far shows that all three reward versions are usable, although v6 is slightly behind the others. Training on the instruct model is now viewed as not meaningful, so future runs will move to the base model. In parallel, we worked on replacing the problematic eurus dataset with stronger alternatives; several datasets have already been swapped in, and experiments are now running to measure their impact.

## Next Week's Plan

Next week, we will complete the dataset replacement experiments and choose one dependable dataset. We will also keep working on reward design, with the goal of producing stable rewards.
