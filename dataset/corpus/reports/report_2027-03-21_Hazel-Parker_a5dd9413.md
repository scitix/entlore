## This Week's Work

Inference modeling progressed with updates to the beleara methodology and February/March experiment set. The team also worked with oliiara project users who are applying beleara, resolved existing simulator problems, and corrected calculation errors across several simulation metrics.

The traffic generator was revised to better represent concurrent traffic patterns, while the PD separation scenario gained SGLang PD dual-end synchronization support. We organized bench_serving validation data for four scenarios and, during simulation validation, identified and removed a high-concurrency SGLang detokenizer bottleneck.

## Next Week's Plan

Next week, the team will consolidate the current methodology and Use Case materials, then finish the Ablation experiments, performance experiments, and article writing.
