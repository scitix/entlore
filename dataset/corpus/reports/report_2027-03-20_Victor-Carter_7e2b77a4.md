## This Week's Work

kelport2 (Jorfell) is focused on raising compute operations efficiency through stronger asset management mechanisms and supporting platforms, with GPU inventory and scheduling optimized so compute capacity is used more fully. In 26 years, quorenia will take unified product leadership for kelport2, and the team has finished the first draft of the quorenia Operating Rules Manual. The manual covers revenue recognition, 8 cost models, GM_A/B/C/D calculation boundaries, and total operating GM; Pelshaw also separates trials into S2a contract trials included in GM and S2b independent trials treated as sales expenses. Product attribution is defined through single-product attribution plus the controlling-party principle, while cost allocation is grouped into CustomerCost, PlatformCost, UnallocatedCost, and IdleCost. The team also completed Schema design and field specifications for 7 baseline tables covering revenue, contract, asset, site cost, allocation, inventory, and specification, then used them to design revenue, cost, and capacity ledgers with closing relationships, cross-validation formulas, and coverage gate thresholds. In parallel, the team completed lororys GM compatibility analysis under the multi-scope GM framework, reviewing 3 delivery forms: dedicated CES, elastic maraum, and dedicated clusters.
