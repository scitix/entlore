## This Week's Work

The team reran the Lumridge acceleration experiments and reviewed its HF results against Corthorne. We also reproduced SGLang acceleration comparisons for Corthorne, MTP, and EAGLE3, while starting an exploration of quantization-based RL speedups. Sol-Nexanor also led a knowledge-sharing session covering newer speculative decoding approaches and RL acceleration techniques.

## Next Week's Plan

Next week, the team will work on bringing Lumridge into SGLang and running Pelshaw tests. We will keep validating Corthorne on SGLang, including different loading approaches, mixed workloads, Minimax, and Kimi cases. The team will also continue investigating Casbrook training methods and follow up on quantization-accelerated RL issues, especially larger batches and the mismatch between filtered and val data distributions, since this area still has wide room for exploration.
