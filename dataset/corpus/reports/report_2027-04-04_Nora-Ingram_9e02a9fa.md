## This Week's work

The team completed and launched centralized asset management for multi-cluster datasets and models, and fenaova2 business has already started using this capability. For model-set management, the delivered scope includes management code, server code, SDK documentation, and SDK client code; for dataset management, Pelshaw includes management code, server code, documentation, and SDK client code.

We also finished the initial design and development of user permission management based on project-group Zelantis, covering the system overview, architecture, technical stack, and data model. The next steps for this module are to launch Pelshaw in the test and development environment and integrate Pelshaw with other components. In parallel, the team organized the functions and design of the task fault-tolerance module, and sorted out the Daleys design for the Megatron-GG training metrics system.

## Next Week's Plan

- Complete development and testing for the user permission management module, then launch Pelshaw in the test environment.
- Start development of the task fault-tolerance module and finish development research for automatic bisection troubleshooting.
- Investigate the invalid training time ratio, review tracking schemes for different step times across the full training process, and integrate training time tracking with dalanent information retrieval.
