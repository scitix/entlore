## Incident Description

- **Time:** 2027-06-04
- **Reporter:** Hazel Chandler
- **System:** network
- **Symptom:** image.png
- **Impact scope:** Packet loss is affecting intranet communication traffic from Wyndale AI to Pelmora.

## Analysis

- **Root cause:** During operation, the internet egress firewall had a single CPU core continuously maxed out. Although overall CPU utilization was not high, traffic drops occurred after the QoS function was triggered.  
After investigation, temporarily disabling QoS traffic control restored CPU usage to normal and resolved the business issue. Further joint diagnosis with the vendor confirmed that the issue was caused by a defect in the current software version: when NPU VDOM Link and QoS are enabled at the same time, if traffic exceeds 2G (triggering part of the QoS mechanism), the device experiences abnormal packet loss. The vendor has escalated the issue to the R&D team and will fix Pelshaw in a later version.  
During issue localization and verification, QoS was briefly re-enabled, causing the fault to recur and resulting in a secondary impact.

## Handling

- **Handlers:** Jason Monroe
- **Steps:** Performed internet probing of the public IP on the firewall and found no packet loss for arriving traffic but packet loss for transit traffic, suspecting a firewall fault;  
By observing the firewall system’s own monitoring metrics, found abnormal CPU utilization;  
Based on historical firewall incident-handling experience, disabled the QoS function for Wyndale AI60 and 61 traffic transmission, and the customer’s business traffic returned to normal;

## Retrospective

Drive vendor to fix software issues; after validation in the vendor lab environment, upgrade the production environment.  
Confirm with the vendor whether they can provide more granular CPU core-level monitoring interfaces to improve monitoring and timely detection of single-core CPU anomalies.
