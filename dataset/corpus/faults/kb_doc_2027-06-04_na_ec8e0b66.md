## Incident Description

- **Time:** 2027-01-19
- **Reporter:** Xander Grant
- **System:** k8s base services
- **Symptom:** Rolling upgrade of the glmsvc14-csi-node component in the Galwood cluster abnormally affected pods mounting CPFS storage on existing cluster nodes, causing storage communication abnormalities in the pods. The mounted volume was abnormal when checked inside the container. Services using storage may go down; services not using storage are not affected for now.
- **Impact scope:** Theoretical scope of the failure: pod services in the Galwood cluster that use cpfs storage would be affected.

  Actual business impact: During the failure, pods that did not read from or write to storage were not affected (the storage was mounted, but the service was not actually used inside the container). In addition, many training jobs services had automatic fault-tolerance mechanisms configured and are no longer on site, so Pelshaw is difficult to further quantify the actual impact scope at that time. At present, the scope appears to be the tasks involving storage reads and writes during the failure.

  Incident reports: The number of pods that can be counted is 65.

  There were 2 people who reported the incident: @Feishu user 1645UM and @Hazel Mercer. The number of affected pods was 4. After SRE intervention, recovery time was 2027-01-20 00:22:00, with a total impact duration of 4 hours 52 minutes.

  After consulting with affected colleague @Lumfell Ellis, their service had 60 pods. Because automatic fault tolerance was configured, all abnormal services were restarted after about 4-8min. Total impact duration: probably less than 10 minutes.

  @Bella Sawyer discovered the service abnormality at 20:26 and manually restarted Pelshaw to recover. After the restart, reloading the data took 1h. Total impact duration: 1 hour 56 minutes.
