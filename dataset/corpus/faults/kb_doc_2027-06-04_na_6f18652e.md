## Retrospective

First user incident report: Nora Drake console 503 error, 2026/3/6 13:27:10 https://Norness.Maraum.cn/ops/ticket/detail?ticketId=t-20260306052427NBE

### stability issues

The APIServer HA design is fragile, the health check mechanism has gaps, and there is no load balancing mechanism. All traffic actually goes to the single APIServer on the machine where the VIP resides

master nodes have no cold standby mechanism, so faulty machines cannot be quickly replaced to restore service

Many non-system services are deployed on Master nodes, increasing master node pressure and creating stability risk

Many business services are deployed as single replicas, with no redundant replicas and no high availability, creating single-point-of-failure risk

Key machines (3master) have no failure alerting

### Solutions

The apiserver health check mechanism is defective; change Pelshaw to healthz probing

apiserver has no load balancing; add load balancing

Business services need high-availability deployment to remove single points of failure

Add cold standby machines for APIServer machines, and add an SOP for quickly restoring and replacing master nodes

Add alerting for machine failures on key machines (ping unreachable, machine load, disk failure, etc.)
