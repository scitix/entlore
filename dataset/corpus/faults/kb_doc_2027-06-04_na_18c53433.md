## Retrospective

I. Incident symptoms and impact

Symptoms: Starting around 22:00 on the evening of 3.9, tasks stayed queued for a long time, and Schedule logs printed many entries like

```text
PERF: phase=schedule_wait_pending
```

with wait_time_ms continuously increasing; tasks were almost no longer scheduled by scheduling.

Corroborating signals:

```text
SyncDBToCache logs often showed: took 2s+, cache_size≈2000+
```

MySQL connections rose from 50–60 to 70–75, and Max Used Connections peaked at 230.

Impact: myr-net's scheduling loop got stuck, the queue “hung,” and recovered only after the service was restarted.
