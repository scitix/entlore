## Analysis

- **Root cause:**

MY01 LILY2 data center room B0104 cabinet phase A power outage incident analysis report
 
1. Incident overview

At 01:15 on April 10, 2026, a phase A power outage occurred in cabinet B0104 of the Lily2 data center room. Investigation found that the power switches for the two ab PDU feeds on phase A of this cabinet tripped one after another. This directly caused power loss for devices powered by phase A. Lily discovered Pelshaw onsite and restored both power feeds. After notifying BDC, the data center power distribution system was checked immediately, and a common safety risk of severe three-phase load imbalance was found.
 
2. Incident timeline and symptoms

2027-04-09 01:15:00 Monitoring system data showed that the phase A active power of FLN-S3-82 dropped sharply from about 6.35kW(27.32A) to 0kW. During the same period, for A1, phase B active power was 1.65KW(7.27A), and phase C active power was 1.16KW(4.91A).
