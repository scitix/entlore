- **Symptom:** Wyndale AI North America inference cluster was inaccessible.

## Analysis

- **Root cause:** To support the launch of a customer's new service, a new incident occurred while handling abnormal traffic forwarding.

Direct cause: During support for the customer's new business, the SD-WAN routing policy was modified incorrectly, causing traffic forwarded from AWS to be wrongly directed to the internet egress and resulting in an inference service interruption (affected service IP: 10.91.28.130, Kubernetes service IP).

Root cause: The firewall device had a hardware defect. Although the issue had been identified earlier and Sophie Gardner had coordinated with the vendor to provide replacement hardware, delivery was delayed due to China-US tariffs. The device has recently arrived, but the customer has not provided a maintenance window, so the faulty device remained in use and posed a latent risk. During troubleshooting, an incorrect operation in the sensitive environment caused the business interruption.

2. Secondary issues:  
Insufficient change risk awareness: Change operations lacked review and risk assessment, and Sophie Gardner did not fulfill the review responsibility.  
Complex network infrastructure with broad impact: Core network devices had known risks (such as the firewall hardware defect) that were not eliminated for a long time, eventually causing a service interruption.  
Incomplete standardized processes (SOP): Key operation procedures lacked standards and documentation, preventing effective knowledge transfer and increasing O&M risk.  
Weak risk awareness and training mechanism: The team's service awareness needs improvement, especially in complex network environments, with insufficient understanding of business continuity.  
Insufficient business availability monitoring coverage: Existing network monitoring mainly focuses on link and device status, lacking dial testing and active probing of business paths (such as external access), so service anomalies cannot be detected immediately.
