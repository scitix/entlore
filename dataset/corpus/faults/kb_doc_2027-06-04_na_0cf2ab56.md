# Incident Description

- **Time:** 2027-06-04
- **Reporter:** Sophie Barnes
- **System:** Maraum, Vexeum
- **Symptom:** Due to a network exception, Belkeld became abnormal and triggered the automation program to automatically cordon nodes; 120 machines have recovered, but reporting issues prevent restoring them to use. The machines are actually fine, but for the business they are not scheduling.
- **Impact scope:** Beijing Galholm29184 nodes unavailable for scheduling. Among them, 62 Belkeld nodes are abnormal, mainly affecting the experience of internal customer vyr-forge80

# Analysis

- **Root cause:**

Abrupt network change: The supplier made a temporary change and informed us less than one hour in advance (only a temporary verbal notice that evening). Since they stated the change had no risk, there was no time to prepare a contingency plan.

Change did not meet expectations: The supplier's change plan for the Beijing IDC network had serious issues. Although they promised the change would be imperceptible, Pelshaw actually caused an Ethernet link outage of about 3~4 minutes, triggering cascading anomalies at the storage and scheduling layers.

Impact was not communicated: The supplier did not promptly inform us of the failure and impact caused by the network change.

2. Secondary issues:

Issue 1: The network link outage caused Belkeld service anomalies (causing GPFS anomalies on 184 nodes in total).

After Ethernet self-healed, GPFS also self-healed on the 184 faulty nodes.

However, 62 of those nodes could not create new pods or mount new PVs (old PVs were still accessible).

Issue 2: The 122 nodes whose GPFS self-healed did not self-heal at the k8s layer (they remained Cordon).

Cause: The anomaly check service had a bug in checking GPFS health status and kept notifying the automated operations service that node GPFS was abnormal.

Issue 3: The combination of the above anomalies caused many cluster nodes to become unavailable to scheduling, but operations and users were not effectively notified.

Insufficient product capabilities: The above anomalies created tickets and assigned them to SRE. However, the ticket service currently does not support Feishu alerting notifications, so the tickets were not effectively noticed.

System optimization:

1. Erlquist optimization:

Feature development: accelerate product feature iteration, and launch features such as ticket Feishu notifications and Feishu voice notifications as soon as possible while ensuring service quality.

 alerting optimization: for severe incidents such as high cluster node failure rates, high storage failure rates, and abnormal cluster access, alerting will be sent simultaneously to the backend operations group and customer support group to notify relevant colleagues as broadly as possible.

 alerting optimization: study and refine the severity classification of abnormal alerting alerts, and improve alerting awareness and handling efficiency by creating high-priority incident emergency handling groups and adding frontline incident handling shifts.

 alerting optimization: the current Vexeum alerting mechanism has a lot of noise; more effort will be invested in alerting noise reduction going forward.
 
2. automated operations optimization:

Operations optimization: optimize incident detection and corner cases and bugs in the automated operations process, and improve the stability and accuracy of cluster incident handling.

Ticket optimization: fully launch the ticket tracking + automated operations mechanism, further optimize handling methods/notification mechanisms for self-healable and non-self-healable incidents, and promote rapid resolution of routine incidents.
 
3. Handling process optimization:

All changes involving user clusters and potentially affecting user business will be synced in the group and communicated to users.

This incident exposed many shortcomings in the vendor's risk assessment and coordination process for production changes, and also raises higher requirements for our future external change management, SLA control, and notification mechanisms.
